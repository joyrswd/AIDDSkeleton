# Products Lifecycle

- When formalizing adopted code/tools, create a project-managed formal implementation with appropriate structure/quality/tests rather than depending on a working/reference copy as production source.
- Generated output/cache/disposable test results/build artifacts/installed dependencies stay with the execution unit and normally untracked.
- When generated/execution material may be retained as evidence or used for a verification claim, evaluate `definition/.routes/index.md` for Verification Basis and the responsible retention area before keeping it.
