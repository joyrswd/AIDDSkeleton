# Jobs Retention and Decision Readiness

## Inactive Retention

- Retain `_<purpose>` only for plausible continuing evaluation, decision, follow-up, diagnostic, restart, or reconsideration value. Root Defer is one possible basis, not the definition of `_`; retention does not make work adopted, required, prioritized, or promised.
- An inactive unit may be a file or directory and needs no `INDEX.md`; use a proportional local entry point such as the file itself or local `README.md` when a directory needs one. Preserve enough retained input/context, provenance/scope, retention/evaluation basis, and useful reevaluation/restart condition to understand why it remains.
- Authorized evidence/context gathering happening now is active work and belongs under `+`; do not use `_` as a quieter state for ongoing work.
- When current work materially matches a retained Defer revisit condition, surface the inactive parent for root reassessment at a useful decision point; do not inspect/silently activate descendants while parent remains inactive. Other `_` units likewise require current basis and applicable assessment/decision before activation.
- Root Defer reassessment: Accept now requires applicable authority before active retained work moves/restructures to `+`; Defer again keeps `_` and refreshes materially changed rationale/revisit condition; Reject deletes unless rationale/provenance has independent continuing value, then classify that value under its responsible area.
- Do not scan every inactive item on every task; discovery relevance screening applies.

## Decision Readiness

A candidate/subset is **decision-ready** when:
- major intent + applicable RBs are coherent enough to judge as a unit;
- remaining questions are separable and not expected to invalidate/materially reshape it.

Decision-ready ≠ implementation-entry complete; details that can be decided consistently after adoption may remain open.

- When decision-ready and adoption/formalization is not already authorized, surface candidate + material basis + remaining open questions using root Decision Requests; do not keep elaborating solely to avoid decision.
- A coherent decision-ready subset may be adopted independently; job-level exit does not delay that adoption.

## Retention

- Do not retain runs, caches, dependencies, logs, generated/disposable output, or suggestions merely because they occurred/present; keep only material with continuing evidential/diagnostic/audit/maintenance/decision/reconsideration value.
- Keep justified supporting material inside its owning marked job while it remains jobs responsibility. If no plausible continuing value/evaluation/revisit basis remains, dispose rather than build an indefinite backlog.
- For `_`, keep evaluation/retention basis discoverable; root Defer additionally keeps disposition rationale/revisit condition current.
- Never delete jobs material while a current verified claim materially depends on it unless remaining/replacement VB is sufficient or the claim is downgraded.
- Jobs VB is transitional only while serving active work, immediate handoff, unresolved reconciliation, or a bounded post-work transition with a specific exit event. “Keep for now” / “reverify later” without one is not bounded.
- If the exit event is missed/cancelled/deferred into open-ended dependency, or the claim must outlive transitional responsibility and remaining basis is inadequate, use suitable durable native/external VB, apply cross-area transfer to the minimum material needed, or downgrade the claim.
- For bounded transition, make effective exit event + intended disposition discoverable enough to judge state; no fixed date/ID/metadata file/history archive is required. At the event, retire/replace/re-evaluate VB or downgrade claim.
