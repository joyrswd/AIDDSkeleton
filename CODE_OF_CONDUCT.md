# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AIDD Skeleton respectful, constructive, and welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or preferred tools.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and professionalism;
- focus criticism on ideas, designs, and outcomes rather than people;
- provide context and evidence when raising concerns;
- accept reasonable feedback and corrections;
- respect project scope, contributor time, and repository rules;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language, sustained disruption, publishing private information without permission, and any conduct that would reasonably make participation feel unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other public or private spaces used to represent or maintain AIDD Skeleton.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using the contact information available on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, lock conversations, or take other proportionate action when this code is violated. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

## Good-faith participation

Disagreement is expected in technical work. Clear questions, respectful dissent, and evidence-based criticism are welcome. Enforcement is intended to address harmful conduct, not ordinary differences of opinion or good-faith mistakes.
